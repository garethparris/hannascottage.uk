Hannas Cottage

Hannas Cottage is a 17th century Welsh cottage. Located in the floral village of St. Florence, Pembrokeshire. It has been extended and refurbished into a comfortable homely standard. The living room and kitchen are part of the original building retaining all it’s original warmth and character.
Hannas Cottage sleeps two people. Upstairs comprises a bedroom, bathroom, and sitting area with CD player. The kitchen and lounge, with television and DVD player, can be found downstairs along with additional shower and toilet facilities.
There is a small, secluded patio area with table, chairs and barbecue facilities. All linen and towels are provided and there is off-road parking for one car. Warm and cosy log fires are provided during the early and late season.
Smokers and pets are not permitted.

Hannas Cottage is available all year round, with short breaks available during the off-peak season, October to March.
